description: Generate one photographic contact-sheet image showing the same character from front, profile, rear, and close-up angles.

slots: [outfit, setting, lighting]
---

## Prompt

Create one **photorealistic multi-angle photographic identity sheet**. When a reference image is attached, base the sheet strictly on the uploaded reference image; otherwise base it on the locked character identity. Match the exact real-world appearance of the person: facial structure, proportions, skin texture, age, asymmetry, natural imperfections, hair and body silhouette. The result must look like real photography of a real human, not a digital character or 3D asset.

Use {{setting}}. The subject wears {{outfit}}. The overall feeling is documentary and natural, not stylized or cinematic.

## Layout

Create one clean photo contact sheet containing **two horizontal rows**:

- **Top row:** four equal full-body photographs of the same person, left to right: facing the camera, left-facing profile, right-facing profile, and facing away from the camera.
- **Bottom row:** three close-up photographic portraits of the same person, left to right: facing the camera, left-facing profile, and right-facing profile.

Keep the seven photographs aligned on one consistent neutral background, at the same scale where framing permits, with equal spacing and no overlapping panels. This is one image containing a reference sheet, not seven unrelated portraits.

## Pose and body language

The subject stands naturally and casually, as a real person would when asked to stand still. No exaggerated stance, rigid pose, artificial symmetry, or fashion-editorial posing. Use subtle natural weight distribution and relaxed posture, with shoulders relaxed and arms resting naturally at the sides. Keep the outfit identical in every panel.

## Consistency and accuracy

Maintain strong identity consistency across all seven photographs. Preserve the locked facial anchors, silhouette anchors, natural human asymmetry, realistic proportions, hair texture, hairline, build and posture. The subject must feel like the same person photographed multiple times, not a replicated model. If a reference image is attached, it is the primary identity source; the locked written identity reinforces it, and the reference wins if there is a conflict.

## Lighting and camera

Use {{lighting}} with natural shadows and gentle falloff. Use realistic camera perspective and lens behavior consistently across the sheet. Avoid dramatic, cinematic, beauty-commercial, or stylized lighting.

## Critical constraints

- Not a 3D render
- Not CGI
- Not a game character
- Not stylized
- Not a model turnaround
- Not an illustration
- Not a fashion lookbook
- No labels, watermark, captions, extra people, duplicate limbs, or inconsistent clothing
