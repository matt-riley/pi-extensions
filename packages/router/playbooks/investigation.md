## Playbook: investigation

Copy these steps into your todo list before task-specific items; a step you skip stays with `skip: <reason>`.

1. Read-only. No edits unless asked.
2. Map first (`repo_map`, `code_search`, `find_definition`), then read the files that matter in full.
3. If the answer is a fact you can observe by running something, run it rather than reasoning about it.
4. Cite every claim with `path:line`, a command and its output, or a URL you read this session. Never invent a caller or an API.
5. Label each claim measured, inferred, or guess. Say what you could not confirm.
6. Answer first, then the evidence. Give a recommendation when asked "should we", including "no".
